About Us

South West Taekwondo Academy is a friendly ITF Taekwondo club based across Bristol and South Gloucestershire. We've been building confident, disciplined and happy practitioners for years — from tiny Little Dragons to experienced black belts. Our club is built on warmth, respect, and a genuine love of the art.
