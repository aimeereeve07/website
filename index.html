What is Taekwondo?

Tae — Foot

Meaning "to strike or break with the foot." Taekwondo is renowned for its dynamic kicking techniques — some of the most spectacular in all martial arts.

Kwon — Hand

Meaning "To punch or destroy with the fist." Hand techniques combine precision and power in a balanced, complete system of self-defence.

Do — The Way

Meaning "The art, way or method." Taekwondo is a discipline and way of life, building confidence, respect, and mental strength alongside physical fitness.

Originating in Korea, Taekwondo is one of the world's most widely practised martial arts. It develops coordination, strength, flexibility and self-discipline — benefits that extend far beyond the dojang into everyday life. The ITF style we teach emphasises traditional patterns, sparring, and the tenets of Taekwondo: courtesy, integrity, perseverance, self-control, and indomitable spirit.

ages 4 - 7

Little Dragons Program

We offer a specialist programme for 4-7 year olds, using martial arts as a training platform to lay the foundation for many years of successful Taekwondo training. Each session places an emphasis on fun, friendship and challenging drills to spark the minds of our youngest students.

What They'll Learn

  • Basic strikes, blocks and fundamental techniques
  • Flexibility, fitness and coordination exercises
  • Self-defence awareness in an age-appropriate way
  • Life skills: focus, discipline and listening
  • Team spirit and leadership through group activities
  • Concentration and dedication through structured drills

Our Approach

We understand that children develop at different rates. With this in mind, our unique syllabus is designed so that every student can achieve — while facing their own challenges and triumphs through the process.

To support their practical training, we've introduced a series of worksheets and study aids that students navigate through with the support of parents, carers and instructors alike.

Gradings run every 4 months, giving students regular milestones to work towards and celebrate. The programme is designed for a smooth transition into the junior and full ITF system when they're ready.
